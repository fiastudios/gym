Questions

Before you decide.

Is there a contract?

Monthly memberships are month to month, with 30 days' notice. Yearly is paid once and can be paused once. The seven-day trial ends unless you choose a plan. We do not store a card to start the trial.

I have not trained in years.

Start in Strength Foundations or Mobility. Coaches cue the whole room. The movement screen on day one is there so your first week matches what you can do today, not what you could do at 25.

Can I bring someone?

All Access includes one guest pass a month. Forge+ includes four. Daylight does not include guests. A guest pass is a full day, classes included if there is a spot.

What should I bring?

Shoes you can train in, and a lock if you have one. We have belts, wraps, gloves, mats, and day lockers. Ride shoes are available to borrow.

Can I freeze if I travel or get injured?

Daylight includes one freeze month a year. All Access and Forge+ include two. Medical freezes are handled at the desk and do not use those months.

How old do I have to be?

16, with a parent on the account. The classes and the floor are built for adults. We do not run a kids' program.

Do you train teams or offices?

Groups of eight or more can take a private hour or a club rate on All Access. Write to hello@forgeathletic.com with the size of the group and the days you have.

Ask for a plan.

Tell us which membership you are considering. We will send the details and a time to come in.

Got it.

We will reply within one business day with times for a tour and the plan you asked about.
