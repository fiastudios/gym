Coaches

They learn your name before your barcode.

Four coaches write the program and teach the hours. The desk can book you with any of them. If you are new, start with whoever is on your first screen.

Head of Strength

Maya Chen

Maya rowed at college and for a club for ten years, then spent eight more teaching people how to hinge, squat, and press without turning it into a personality. She runs Foundations for anyone new to a barbell, and a quieter intermediate hour for people who have been under a bar for a while.

  • Strength program
  • Mon, Wed, Fri, Sat, Sun
  • Sessions on the floor

Conditioning & Ride

Jordan Hale

Jordan designs engine work that does not wreck the rest of the week. Intervals, carries, and heart-rate zones in the studio, then the same idea on a bike in a darker room. Classes end on time. The playlist is his, the plan is on the board before you start.

  • Conditioning & ride
  • Most weekdays and Saturday
  • Engine is the sharper hour

Mobility & Recovery

Amira Sol

Amira keeps members training next month, not just today. Hips, shoulders, breathing, and the unglamorous work that makes strength possible. She also runs the recovery suite: heat, cold, and twenty quiet minutes, not a menu of gadgets.

  • Mobility daily
  • Recovery suite
  • Good first hour if you sit all day

Boxing

Leo Park

Leo teaches boxing for people who want the skill, and for people who want the conditioning that comes with learning it. Footwork first. No sparring unless you ask for the Saturday invitational. Wraps and gloves are in the cubbies if you do not own them yet.

  • Boxing technique
  • Weekday evenings & Saturday
  • Gloves to borrow

Private coaching

A coach, not a printout.

One session is an hour on the floor with a plan you leave holding. Forge+ includes two a month. Anyone else can book them at $95, or eight sessions for $720. The first one can happen during your trial if a coach has the hour.

  • $95 a session
  • 8 for $720
  • 2 a month on Forge+
