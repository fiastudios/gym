Address

Hours

The desk is staffed until 8pm. After that, members use a door code.

Parking

The lot behind the building is free for the first hour, then $2. Street parking on Mercer is free after 6pm.

What to bring

Training shoes and a lock if you own one. Belts, wraps, gloves, mats, and day lockers are here. Ride shoes are at the desk.
